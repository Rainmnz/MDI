SOCIAL MEDIA • REACH AND TRAFFIC

Turn attention into a useful next step.

A practical look at creating content, building reach and helping interested people find the information they need.

Views can help content reach people, but views alone are not a business. The more useful questions are whether the content reaches the right audience, communicates clearly and gives people a relevant next step.

Reach

Earn attention

Develop ideas that are relevant to a defined audience and communicate them clearly.

Relevance

Help people understand

Make sure the landing page matches the promise of the content and answers the visitor’s real question.

Action

Measure what matters

Look beyond view counts. Where appropriate, track meaningful engagement and visitor actions while respecting privacy and platform rules.

A repeatable process

  1. Choose a topic and audience.
  2. Publish a clear, useful piece of content.
  3. Review the response and learn from it.
  4. Improve the message and destination page.
  5. Repeat consistently without promising guaranteed results.
