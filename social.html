FINDING CUSTOMERS

Attention is only the beginning.

Finding an opportunity is one thing. Finding the right audience and earning their attention is another.

I’m building practical training around a simple content-led approach to social media. The aim is to explain the process in usable steps rather than promise overnight results.

1 • Create

Make useful content

Start with a clear audience and a specific idea. Make the message easy to understand and worth someone’s time.

2 • Publish

Learn from the response

Test formats and topics, then use actual engagement to understand what resonates. Results vary across platforms and audiences.

3 • Improve

Build a repeatable process

Use what you learn to improve your content, consistency and ability to guide interested people to relevant information.

What the training is intended to cover

  • Choosing topics and shaping short-form content.
  • Creating a simple, repeatable publishing workflow.
  • Understanding attention, engagement and follow-up.
  • Connecting content to a clear next step without misleading claims.
